Required columns and units

Upload a completed IndPenSim-format batch with one row per measurement time. The loader recognizes the source headings below and maps them to MaverickX's internal schema.

Data checks

  • Time must be numeric, unique, and strictly increasing.
  • Required measurements must be numeric. At most two consecutive missing samples inside a series may be linearly interpolated; leading, trailing, or longer gaps are rejected.
  • Irregular sampling intervals are reported as a data-quality warning.
  • Optional process channels are preserved when recognized. Fault labels are metadata and are not model inputs.

Anomaly alerts mean unusual behavior relative to healthy simulator history. They do not establish contamination or a physical cause.
